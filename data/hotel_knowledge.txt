Welcome to the Royal Park Hotel!

- Check-in time is at 2:00 PM.
- Check-out time is at 11:00 AM.
- The hotel offers free Wi-Fi for all guests.
- Our contact phone number is +1 (555) 123-4567.
- Our email is info@royalparkhotel.com.

# Amenities
- We have a rooftop swimming pool that is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Our fitness center is open 24/7 for all guests.
- We offer complimentary breakfast from 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM in the main dining hall.
- Spa services are available on the 2nd floor from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM (reservation required).

# Room Types
- Standard Room: Includes a queen-sized bed, city view, and a minibar.
- Deluxe Room: Includes a king-sized bed, ocean view, and a private balcony.
- Suite: Includes a separate living area, king-sized bed, premium ocean view, and complimentary wine.

# Policies
- Pets are not allowed, with the exception of service animals.
- Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the hotel rooms. A $250 cleaning fee will be charged for smoking in rooms.
- Cancellations made 48 hours prior to the check-in date are fully refundable.
- Late check-out is available upon request and subject to availability (additional fees may apply).
